02 / Project milestones

A journey from question to evidence.

Choose an assessment below to view its purpose, date and allocated marks.

10 marks · 16–18 March 2026 · Completed

Project Proposal

The proposal set the problem, the literature position, the objectives, and a feasible build plan. Written feedback on 2 May 2026 closed the earlier literacy topic. The approved direction is an AI/ML-powered smart farm, with four components and about 3,450–3,500 records from National Livestock Development Board farms.

Proposal slides · Proposal document

15 marks · 11–13 May 2026 · Completed

Progress Presentation 1

Each member showed a working slice: a disease reading, a manual weight that produces a daily ration, a birth or hatch estimate, and a rupee forecast. The review looked for a real component, evidence of data, and a clear individual contribution.

Progress 1 slides

15 marks · 31 August–02 September 2026 · Completed

Progress Presentation 2

The components meet in the Flutter app and the unified API. The demonstration covers a disease reading with severity and treatment, behaviour from video, and at least one of feed, birth, hatch, or market. Reported figures stay as published: DenseNet121 at 99.45% on the disease image set, YOLOv8x near 99%, and behaviour detection near 83–86%.

15 marks · 19–21 October 2026 · In progress

Final Assessment

The final presentation walks a panel through the finished path: identify the animal, diagnose, grade severity, suggest treatment, and show feed, reproduction, or market on the same herd record. This 15 is the demonstration. The written report is marked with the viva.

Final slides

40 marks · viva 19–21 October 2026 · thesis 28 October 2026 · Upcoming

Final Report & Viva

The group report and the four individual reports state the gap, the method, the evaluation, and the limits. That written work is 30 marks. The oral examination is 10. Each member defends the component they built. The app supports a decision and tells the farmer when to call a veterinarian. It does not certify a diagnosis.

Final documents

Dates and status follow the project timeline. Marks follow the group plan: proposal 10, each progress presentation 15, the final demonstration 15, the final report 30, and the viva 10.
