Project proposal

Completed

Marks allocated: 10  ·  When: proposal presentation, with written feedback on 2 May 2026.

The proposal presented the problem, the literature position, objectives, and a feasible build plan. Panel feedback redirected the group. The earlier literacy-assessment idea was closed, and the approved direction became transforming a conventional farm into an intelligent smart farm.

From that date the group fixed individual components: disease detection and emergency alerts, feeding and nutrition, birth and hatch prediction, and milk-market forecasting with cattle identity. Field discussion included a conventional cattle setting associated with the National Livestock Development Board.

Progress presentation 1

Progress cycle

Marks allocated: 15  ·  When: mid-year progress window, after the May redirect.

This review checks that each member has a real component, not only a slide. The panel looks for a working slice of the model, evidence of data, and a clear individual contribution.

  • Disease path: image in, class and confidence out, with the dual-model comparison sketched.
  • Feeding path: a manual weight measurement producing a daily feed figure.
  • Reproduction path: birth and hatch forms returning a probability or a day estimate.
  • Market path: a rupee forecast from price, litres, and quality.

Progress presentation 2

September 2026 window

Marks allocated: 15  ·  When: September 2026 progress window.

The second progress review expects the components to meet. A farmer can open the Flutter app, reach a service, and receive a result from the unified API. Demonstrations at this stage cover a live disease reading with severity and treatment, behaviour or video analysis, and at least one of feed, birth, hatch, or market.

What is being judged is integration and honesty about accuracy: DenseNet121 at 99.45% on the disease image set, YOLOv8x near 99%, behaviour detection near 83–86%, and response times short enough for a phone.

Final assessment

Upcoming

Marks allocated: 45, as the final report (30) and the final presentation (15)  ·  When: closing assessment of the 2025/26 research year.

The report states the gap, the method, the models, the evaluation, and the limits. The presentation walks a panel through the finished path: identify the animal, diagnose, grade severity, suggest treatment, and show feed, reproduction, or market on the same herd record.

Individual reports sit with the group report. The repository, this website, and the logbook are the evidence trail.

Viva

Upcoming

Marks allocated: 10  ·  When: oral examination with the final assessment.

Each member defends the component they built. Expected questions cover why two vision models are compared, which features drive severity, how a video frame is sampled, what the hatch inputs mean, and where the rupee forecast can mislead a farmer.

The standing answer on clinical use: the app supports a decision and tells the farmer when to call a veterinarian. It does not certify a diagnosis.
