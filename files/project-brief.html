Project charter summary

Converting a traditional farm into an AI-driven smart farm.

Faculty of Computing, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology. Academic year 2025/26. Project ID R26-IT-093.

Purpose

Give a cattle farmer one phone path for the decisions that currently depend on a late look: naming a visible disease, grading how serious it is, suggesting a treatment to discuss with a veterinarian, planning feed, anticipating birth or hatch, and estimating next month’s milk income in rupees.

Scope

  • Eight visual classes, two vision models, higher-confidence selection.
  • Severity and treatment from clinical numbers.
  • Behaviour from stills and video, with baseline alerts.
  • Manual weight measurement, daily feed, and nutrition.
  • Calving window and egg-hatch probability.
  • Milk price and income forecast.
  • Cattle identity and a herd record.

Proposal position

On 2 May 2026 the proposal panel’s feedback closed an earlier literacy-assessment topic. The group adopted this farming study, split the work into individual components, and continued against a conventional cattle setting discussed with the National Livestock Development Board.
