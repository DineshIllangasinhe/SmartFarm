Project documentation

Converting a traditional farm into an AI/ML-powered automated smart farm.

Faculty of Computing, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology. Academic year 2025/26. Project ID R26-IT-093. Names and numbers follow the topic assessment form.

Group

  • IT22136138 — Jayawardhana H.A.S. Automated feeding and nutrition: cameras for type, size, and health, a manual weight measurement, and a ration the system can dispense.
  • IT22282118 — Illangasinhe I.M.A.D. Disease detection and emergency alerts: cameras and sensors for abnormal behaviour or symptoms, a first recommendation, or a call to a veterinarian.
  • IT22139894 — Shamika G.L. Animal birth and egg hatch prediction from image, sensor, and historical data.
  • IT22134608 — Arachchi W.A.A.I.W. Customized live market prediction for selling and buying, with an AI price forecast.

Shared farm records are about 3,450–3,500 rows from National Livestock Development Board farms. Disease images include a Kaggle set.

Purpose

Give a cattle farmer one phone path for the decisions that currently depend on a late look or a habit: naming a visible disease, grading how serious it is, suggesting a treatment to discuss with a veterinarian, planning feed from a measured weight, setting a nutrition plan, anticipating birth or hatch, and estimating next month’s milk income in Sri Lankan rupees.

The system is decision support. It does not replace a veterinarian. A severe or contagious result is meant to start a call, not to close the case.

What the project includes

  • Eight visual classes — contagious disease, dermatophilosis, foot-and-mouth disease, healthy, lumpy skin, mastitis, pediculosis, and ringworm — with the higher-confidence result kept from DenseNet121 and YOLOv8x.
  • Severity graded mild, moderate, or severe, and a treatment suggestion from clinical features such as measured weight, age, and temperature.
  • Nine cattle behaviours from stills and video, with alerts when a pattern leaves a normal range or that animal’s baseline.
  • Manual weight measurement. The farmer enters a scale or tape reading. Daily feed then follows that weight, breed, age, milk yield, and activity.
  • A nutrition plan from health and production inputs, including dry-matter intake and minerals.
  • A calving window, and an egg-hatch probability from incubation temperature, humidity, egg weight, turning, and days set.
  • A next-month forecast of milk price change, price per litre, and income in rupees.
  • Cattle identity, so a detection, a feed, a birth, a hatch, or a market check stays on one herd record.

Manual weight measurement

Body weight is an input the farmer measures and types in. Photographs in this project are used for disease, behaviour, and identity. The feed model and the nutrition model read the entered weight together with production and health fields. Severity and treatment also receive that measured weight as a clinical number.

How a result is produced

  1. The Flutter app collects a photograph, a short video, or a form. A weight field accepts the manual measurement.
  2. The Flask API on port 5000 runs the matching model. Disease detection compares DenseNet121 and YOLOv8x and returns the stronger call, then gradient boosting grades severity and suggests treatment.
  3. Behaviour detection uses YOLOv8s. Birth, hatch, nutrition, and milk income use tabular models.
  4. Laravel stores users, cows, and prediction history so the next decision has a record.
