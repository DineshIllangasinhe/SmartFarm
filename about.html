About us

A computing group working on a farmer’s week.

We are undergraduates in the Faculty of Computing at SLIIT, project R26-IT-093, Converting a traditional farm into an AI/ML-powered automated smart farm. Ms. Manori Gamage supervises the research, with Ms. Thilini Jayalath as co-supervisor. The group is Jayawardhana H.A.S., Illangasinhe I.M.A.D., Shamika G.L., and Arachchi W.A.A.I.W.

How the work is divided

Illangasinhe I.M.A.D. · IT22282118

Disease detection and emergency alerts

Cameras and sensors score behaviour and visible symptoms. The system recommends a first action or alerts a veterinarian.

Jayawardhana H.A.S. · IT22136138

Automated feeding and nutrition

Manual weight measurement, camera checks of type and size, and a health-aware ration the system can dispense.

Shamika G.L. · IT22139894

Birth and egg hatch prediction

Image, sensor, and historical records estimate a healthy calf birth or a successful hatch.

Arachchi W.A.A.I.W. · IT22134608

Live market prediction

An up-to-date selling and buying record, with an AI price forecast and a buy or sell recommendation.

Names and registration numbers follow the topic assessment form for R26-IT-093. Farm records are about 3,450–3,500 rows from National Livestock Development Board farms.
