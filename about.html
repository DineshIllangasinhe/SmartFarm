How the work is divided

Component

Disease detection and emergency alerts

Dual vision models, severity, treatment, behaviour baselines, and video timelines.

Component

Feeding and nutrition

Manual weight measurement, a daily feed amount, and a health-aware nutrient recommendation.

Component

Birth and hatch prediction

Near-term calving, and hatch probability from incubation conditions.

Component

Market, identity, and the herd record

Next-month milk income in rupees, cattle detection, and the record that ties a result to an animal.
